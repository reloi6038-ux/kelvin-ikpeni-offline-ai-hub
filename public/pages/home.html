The Access Gap

For too many students, learning ends when the internet does

Four barriers block millions of students from continuous learning — the platform addresses all four.

01
💰

Data is expensive

A single hour-long video lesson can cost more than a student's daily transport. Downloading content repeatedly is out of reach.

02
📵

Connectivity is unreliable

Power cuts and dead zones mean lessons end mid-sentence. Students lose progress, momentum, and trust in online learning.

03
📱

Devices are basic and shared

Low-RAM Android phones can't handle heavy apps or video. Students often share one phone with siblings.

04
📚

Materials don't match the curriculum

Generic AI answers ignore WAEC, NECO, and national syllabi — leaving students with content that doesn't help them pass exams.

The Solution

Download once. Learn forever.

Every feature is designed to keep that promise.

⬇

Download Learning Packs

Complete lessons, quizzes, and pre-generated AI explanations bundled into a single lightweight pack — usually under 500 KB.

✨

AI Tutor, Offline

Explain simply, give examples, summarize, or quiz you — with no internet. Answers come from your downloaded curriculum, not generic guesses.

📚

Local Curriculum First

Grounded in Nigerian, WAEC, NECO, and IGCSE syllabi. Administrators can import custom curriculum files from the browser.

📶

Low-Data Mode

One switch disables images, animations, and remote fonts. Text-only mode strips everything but pure HTML.

🎓

Adaptive Learning

Quiz performance quietly teaches the platform which topics need more practice — so revision is targeted, not generic.

🔒

Safe, Verified AI

Every AI answer shows its source. Curriculum content is labeled separately from AI-generated suggestions. Nothing is presented as fact without grounding.

👥

Local Classroom Mode

One device becomes a hotspot learning server. Nearby students join with their own phones — no individual data needed.

🧠

Quiz & Learn Offline

Complete quizzes offline. Feedback is instant. Progress saves locally and syncs automatically when you reconnect.

🌍

Built for Students

English-first with room for Nigerian and African languages. Optional text-to-speech. Works on ₦15,000 Android phones.
