Help & Project Info

How Kelvin Hub works, why it exists, and how to demo it.

🎯 What is Kelvin Hub?

Kelvin Hub is an offline-first AI-assisted learning platform for students in underserved communities. Download a complete learning pack once — lessons, quizzes, AI explanations — then keep learning with zero internet.

Built for the challenge "AI for Zero-Bandwidth & Localized Learning" under the theme "AI for the Next Generation: Reimagining How Students Learn, Work & Grow".

🚀 Try the 5-minute demo

  1. Download a pack. Go to Download Center → click ⬇ on "JSS2 Mathematics Essentials" → wait for "Downloaded" badge.
  2. Toggle Demo Offline. Click the purple pill in the bottom-right corner. The header badge flips to "Demo Offline".
  3. Open My Library. Click a lesson — it reads instantly from your device.
  4. Ask the AI tutor. Click "Explain Simply" — an answer appears from the downloaded AI cache.
  5. Take a quiz. Go to Quizzes → answer questions → get instant feedback.
  6. Toggle Demo Offline off. Watch the pending-sync card disappear in under a second. Your attempt syncs to the server.
  7. See your dashboard. Visit Dashboard — streak, weak topics, and history update live.

🧠 How does the AI work offline?

The AI layer is a three-tier fallback system, so it never fails and never invents facts:

  1. Cache — pre-generated AI responses bundled into the learning pack. Instant, works offline.
  2. Provider — when online and configured, the server calls Gemini/OpenAI/DeepSeek for a live answer.
  3. Template — deterministic responses built from the lesson's keyPoints. Always works, always grounded.

Every response is labeled with its source: "From curriculum notes" (yellow), "AI-generated" (blue), or "Verified" (green). Students always know what they're looking at.

📱 Works offline?

Yes. After downloading a pack once, the following work with zero network:

  • Lesson reading
  • AI tutor (Explain, Examples, Practice, Summarize)
  • Free-form questions (keyword match fallback)
  • Quizzes and instant feedback
  • Content search
  • Student dashboard (streaks, weak topics, history)
  • Pending sync queue — auto-syncs when online

The Admin dashboard requires internet — that's by design.

🔒 Privacy & Safety

  • No accounts required for students — anonymous learning is supported.
  • Local-first data — progress lives in IndexedDB on your device, only synced on demand.
  • No third-party fonts, analytics, or trackers — the entire app is self-contained.
  • AI answers show sources — students can distinguish curriculum content from AI suggestions.
  • Template fallback never fabricates — it echoes the lesson or says "not in downloaded notes".

❓ Troubleshooting

The page shows "404 — Page not found"

That page hasn't been built yet. It will appear as the next build steps complete. Currently available pages:

  • Home
  • Courses
  • AI Tutor
  • Download Center
  • My Library
  • Quizzes
  • Dashboard
  • Admin
  • Help
The AI tutor doesn't respond

Two possibilities:

  • No lessons downloaded — the tutor only works from downloaded packs. Visit Download Center and download one.
  • No AI cache in the pack — the pack was built before AI responses were generated. Visit the Admin dashboard and click "Warm AI cache" for each lesson, then rebuild the pack.
My quiz attempt didn't sync

Attempts queue in your browser's IndexedDB when offline and sync automatically when you reconnect. If sync seems stuck:

  • Check the connection badge — is it Online?
  • Visit Dashboard — is the "Pending sync" card visible? Click "Sync now".
  • Open DevTools → Application → IndexedDB → kelvin_hub_offline → attempts. If items are there with synced: false, the sync is pending.
The page doesn't update when I download a pack

Refresh the page. The Download Center reads from IndexedDB on load, and the tab that performed the download doesn't automatically refresh other tabs.

The admin dashboard says "Admin API is disabled"

The ENABLE_ADMIN env variable must be true. Add it to .env, restart the server.
